## 01. Chinese Brands

- **中文题目**：中国品牌
- **category**：中国发展与中国品牌
- **sourceType**：verified-recall
- **tags**：Chinese brands, innovation, quality, global market
- **questions**：
  - Why are Chinese brands becoming more popular?
  - How can Chinese brands become more competitive internationally?
  - What do you think of products made in China?
  - Why are young consumers more willing to choose Chinese brands?

### 中文逻辑骨架

1. 中国品牌近年明显更受欢迎。
2. 企业越来越重视质量、设计和技术创新。
3. 年轻消费者对国产品牌更自信。
4. 未来仍需提升国际声誉和售后服务。
5. 长期竞争力来自质量和创新，而不只是低价。

### 90 秒准备稿

In recent years, Chinese brands have become much more popular, both in China and overseas.

In my opinion, there are two main reasons for this change. First, many Chinese companies are paying more attention to quality and innovation. Instead of simply offering low prices, they are developing new technologies and improving the design of their products. Chinese brands in areas such as smartphones, electric vehicles and home appliances have become increasingly competitive.

Second, young consumers are becoming more confident about products made in China. They care more about quality, design and personal experience than whether a brand comes from a foreign country.

Of course, Chinese brands still need to improve their international reputation and after-sales services.

Overall, I believe Chinese brands have made great progress, and they will become even more competitive in the global market.

- **中文题目**：人工智能与就业
- **category**：科技与人工智能
- **sourceType**：historical-topic
- **tags**：AI, employment, technology, future jobs
- **questions**：
  - Will AI cause mass unemployment?
  - How will AI influence future jobs?
  - Is AI a threat or an opportunity?
  - How should university students prepare for the age of AI?

### 中文逻辑骨架

1. AI 正迅速改变就业市场。
2. 部分重复性工作可能被替代。
3. 新技术同时也会创造新岗位。
4. 创造力、沟通和判断仍难被机器取代。

### 90 秒准备稿

However, this is only one side of the story. New technologies also create new kinds of jobs. Today, we need people who can develop, manage and apply AI systems. More importantly, abilities such as creativity, communication and human judgment are still difficult for machines to replace.

So instead of simply being afraid of AI, we should learn how to work with it.

For university students, the real challenge is not whether AI will arrive, because it already has. The challenge is whether we can develop the skills needed in this new environment.

I also think a good team does not mean that everyone always agrees. Different opinions can actually lead to better ideas if people are willing to listen to each other.

In short, cooperation allows us to achieve things that would be much harder to accomplish alone.

- **tags**：trust, honesty, responsibility, relationships

5. 信任来自长期稳定的行为。

### 90 秒准备稿

Trust is one of the most important parts of any relationship, whether it is between friends, classmates or colleagues.

In my opinion, the first way to gain other people's trust is to be honest. If we make a promise, we should try our best to keep it. If we cannot do something, it is better to say so clearly than to make an unrealistic promise.

Second, we should be responsible. A trustworthy person does not disappear when a problem occurs. Instead, he or she tries to solve the problem and accepts responsibility for mistakes.

Another important point is consistency. People usually trust us because of what we do repeatedly, not because of one impressive action.

Trust takes a long time to build but can be lost very quickly.

Therefore, if we want others to trust us, we should be honest, responsible and consistent in our daily behavior.

### 90 秒准备稿

Stress is a common part of university life. Students may worry about exams, future careers, relationships or whether they are making good use of their time.

I don't think all stress is bad. A reasonable amount of pressure can motivate us to work harder. The problem begins when stress becomes too strong or lasts for too long.

When I feel stressed, one useful method is to divide a difficult task into several smaller steps. This makes the problem feel much more manageable.

Exercise and proper rest are also important. Sometimes studying for another two hours is less effective than taking a short break and coming back with a clearer mind.

Talking to friends can also help because we often discover that other people are facing similar problems.

For me, managing stress does not mean avoiding challenges. It means learning how to face them in a healthier and more effective way.

Of course, the role of libraries is changing. Today, a good university library is not only a place for borrowing books. It also provides digital resources, study spaces and academic services.

## 08. Machine Translation

- **中文题目**：机器翻译
- **category**：科技与人工智能
- **sourceType**：historical-topic
- **tags**：translation, AI, language, technology
- **questions**：
  - Can machine translation replace human translators?
  - What are the advantages of machine translation?
  - What are the limitations of machine translation?
  - What will the future of translation look like?

### 中文逻辑骨架

1. 机器翻译发展快，而且非常方便。
2. 日常场景下速度快、成本低。
3. 但语言与文化、语境紧密相关。
4. 幽默、情绪和文化含义仍很难准确处理。
5. 未来更可能是人机协作。

### 90 秒准备稿

Machine translation has developed rapidly in recent years and has made communication much more convenient.

For everyday situations, it is extremely useful. If we want to understand a foreign website, travel in another country or communicate with someone who speaks a different language, machine translation can give us an answer almost immediately.

However, I don't think it can completely replace human translators.

Language is closely connected with culture and context. The same sentence may have different meanings in different situations. Humor, emotion and cultural references are also difficult for machines to understand perfectly.

This is especially important in literature, international business and other professional situations where small mistakes may cause serious misunderstandings.

Therefore, I think the future of translation will probably involve cooperation between humans and machines.

Machines can provide speed and efficiency, while humans can provide judgment, accuracy and cultural understanding.
